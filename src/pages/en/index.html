Advancing vaccines for healthier aquaculture.

AquaVant develops MicroVant, a series of adjuvants that help fish vaccines work. We supply them to the companies that make the vaccines.

Explore the technology Partner with us

We are an Oslo biotechnology company focused on one part of the fish vaccine: the adjuvant. It is the ingredient that helps a vaccinated fish’s immune system respond, and the field our founders have worked in for more than 45 combined years.

We do not make vaccines. We develop ready-to-use adjuvant formulations for the companies that do, and support them with advice on vaccine production, vaccination equipment and clinical trials.

Our first formulations are built for salmonids and tilapia, two of the largest groups of farmed fish.

About AquaVant
Why it matters

Healthy fish need vaccines that work.

Disease is one of the biggest challenges in fish farming. Vaccines help prevent it, and the adjuvant is a critical part of every injectable fish vaccine.

  • Disease pressure

    Preventing disease remains a major challenge for aquaculture worldwide.

  • Antibiotic use

    Where vaccines are hard to get, many markets still rely heavily on antibiotics.

  • Few specialists

    Adjuvant supply for fish vaccines is fragmented, so manufacturers have few dedicated partners to turn to.

How we work

We work through vaccine manufacturers.

Our customers are the companies that make fish vaccines. A partnership usually follows four steps.

  1. Talk

    We start with your species, vaccine format and manufacturing needs.

  2. Evaluate

    Joint formulation work and testing in your own R&D.

  3. Qualify

    Documentation, quality control and a secure supply chain.

  4. Supply

    A commercial agreement for delivery of the adjuvant.

Services

Expertise around the whole vaccine.

Beyond the adjuvant, we advise manufacturers on vaccine production, on equipment for manual and automatic vaccination, and on clinical trials in the lab and in the field.
