Specialists in fish vaccine adjuvants.

AquaVant AS is an Oslo-based biotechnology and aquaculture company, founded by people who have spent their careers on fish vaccines.

Our ambition is to become the dedicated adjuvant partner for fish vaccine manufacturers worldwide. Better vaccines mean healthier fish, and less need for antibiotics in aquaculture.

Background

Why we started AquaVant.

Vaccines are one of the most important tools for keeping farmed fish healthy. The adjuvant is a critical part of an injectable fish vaccine, yet few suppliers specialise in adjuvants made for fish.

Our founders saw that gap after decades of developing and registering fish vaccines for global animal-health companies. AquaVant was set up to fill it: a company that focuses on the adjuvant, and works through the manufacturers that make the vaccines.

We are based in Oslo, close to one of the world’s leading aquaculture industries, and work with customers internationally.
