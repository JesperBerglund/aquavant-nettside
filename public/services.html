Expertise around the whole vaccine.

The adjuvant is our product. Around it, we help manufacturers with production, vaccination equipment and clinical trials.

What we offer

One partner for the adjuvant and the know-how around it.

Our founders have spent their careers developing, producing and registering fish vaccines. We put that experience to work for our customers.

MicroVant adjuvants

Ready-to-use adjuvant formulations for injectable fish vaccines, developed for salmonids and tilapia. We work with you on formulation and testing until the adjuvant is qualified for your vaccine.

About the technology

Vaccine production

Advice on producing fish vaccines and on the equipment you need to do it.

  • Production processes and scale-up
  • Choice of production equipment
  • Quality and documentation requirements

Vaccination equipment

Advice on equipment for giving vaccines to fish, by hand or with automatic machines.

  • Manual vaccination
  • Automatic vaccination systems
  • Matching equipment to vaccine and fish size

Clinical trials

Advice on planning and running vaccine trials, in the laboratory and in the field.

  • Trial design
  • Laboratory and field trials
  • Documentation for regulatory submissions

Technical support

Help with putting a new adjuvant into use, from the first formulation trials to routine production.

  • Formulation support
  • Customer R&D testing
  • Ongoing technical follow-up
Who we work with

Built for the companies that make fish vaccines.

  • Global manufacturers

    Animal-health companies with established fish vaccine portfolios.

  • Local producers

    Regional vaccine makers, including producers of autogenous (farm-specific) vaccines.

  • Aquaculture companies

    Fish farmers and partners who want to understand vaccine options.
