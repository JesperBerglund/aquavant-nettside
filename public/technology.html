What an adjuvant does

The antigen shows the target. The adjuvant helps the fish respond.

A vaccine teaches the immune system to recognise a disease. The antigen is the part it learns to recognise. The adjuvant is added so the immune system responds more strongly. In injectable fish vaccines, the adjuvant is critical to how well the vaccine works.

Simplified illustration, not to scale.

How it fits in

How a fish vaccine is made with MicroVant.

AquaVant supplies the adjuvant. The vaccine manufacturer supplies the antigen and makes the vaccine.

  1. AntigenDeveloped and produced by the vaccine manufacturer.
  2. MicroVant adjuvantSupplied by AquaVant, ready to use.
  3. FormulationThe manufacturer combines antigen and adjuvant into the finished vaccine.
  4. VaccinationFish are vaccinated by injection, by hand or with automatic equipment.

Where we start

Salmonids and tilapia.

Two species groups with different needs: one with established vaccine use, one with large volumes and growing vaccine adoption.

Salmonids

Salmon and trout farming has well-established vaccine use. Our founders know the adjuvant composition and documentation these vaccines require. We work with global manufacturers and with makers of autogenous (farm-specific) vaccines.

Tilapia

Tilapia is farmed in large volumes, and vaccine use is increasing. We work with local vaccine producers and larger pharmaceutical companies.
